# Changelog

[简体中文](CHANGELOG.md) | [English](CHANGELOG.en-US.md)

## 1.7.0 (2026-08-29)

### More Streaming Platforms

- Added Twitch and YouTube live rooms with link detection, persistent monitoring, adaptive quality, and real-time danmaku.

### User Experience

- Added a first-run guide covering the six supported platforms, adding streamers, monitoring layouts, audio focus, and danmaku.
- Added Simplified Chinese and English interfaces, selectable in Settings and applied after restart.

### Playback Stability

- Improved multi-stream loading, quality switching, and A/V synchronization to reduce long-session stutter and cross-room interference.
