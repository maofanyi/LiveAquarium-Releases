## 1.13.0 (2026-10-03)

### Bilibili accounts and chat

- Sign in to Bilibili, sync followed channels, and access stream quality options available to your account.
- Send chat messages, identify your own messages, and use +1 replies.

### Douyu gift statistics

- View gift summaries, individual entries, and trends, with time filters and an option to hide gifts with unknown prices.
- Estimate Diamond fan and nobility subscriptions and renewals. Estimates do not represent actual payments or streamer income.
- Statistics stay on your device for today and the preceding two days, expire automatically, and are not uploaded to servers.

### Usability improvements

- Improved group management, member ordering, and title styling.
- Monitoring schemes can save and restore temporary rooms.
- Improved activity list refresh, window interactions, and playback stability.
